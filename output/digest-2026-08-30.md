*Daily digest — 2026-08-30*

_TL;DR: coding-tool model access is becoming a contract risk while agent interfaces expand from software into physical hardware._

1. *Plan now for OpenAI models leaving Cursor on November 12*
   OpenAI proposed ending its model-supply contract with Cursor after its acquisition by SpaceX and says future models will not be provided. Cursor users can still configure direct provider keys for standard models, but specialized features depend on Cursor's built-in integrations.
   Why it matters: teams should test provider fallbacks before the cutoff
   https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/

2. *Apply to test Anthropic's model-agnostic hardware standard*
   Anthropic opened the Model Hardware Standard preview to research labs and manufacturers. It gives agents a common interface for microscopes, liquid handlers, and robotic arms through MCP, CLI, or APIs, with an open-source release planned after safety testing.
   Why it matters: hardware teams can replace bespoke device integrations with a portable agent interface
   https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview

3. *a16z opens a $1.1B fund for AI's physical stack*
   The Machine Age Fund will invest across chips, memory, networking, storage, data centers, robotics, and home AI hardware. a16z says hardware startups now represent more than 20% of its deal flow.
   Why it matters: infrastructure founders have a new dedicated capital pool to approach
   https://a16z.com/the-machine-age-fund/

4. *Save DALL·E GPT assets before today's retirement*
   OpenAI is retiring the official DALL·E GPT in ChatGPT today. ChatGPT Images remains available, and user-created GPTs with image generation are unaffected.
   Why it matters: images kept only in old DALL·E GPT sessions should be exported now
   https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes
