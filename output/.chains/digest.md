*Digest — 2026-08-23*

_TL;DR: The privacy trade gets an institutional bid (Grayscale Zcash ETF), an L1 gives up on itself (BounceBit winds down after a $3M exploit), and Nvidia buys AI coding talent without an acquisition._

1. *Grayscale files 5th amendment for a spot Zcash ETF — would be the first US privacy-coin fund*
   Grayscale moved to convert its $260M+ Zcash Trust into a spot ETF (ticker ZCH, NYSE Arca, 2.5% fee, Coinbase custody). If cleared it's the first US spot Zcash ETF, landing as ZEC prints an 8-year high on the week's privacy rotation.
   Why it matters: the ZEC/privacy run now has a concrete institutional catalyst, not just momentum — ride while the filing advances, but the 2.5% fee and no approval yet argue for fading any parabolic spike into the SEC decision.
   https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001720265/000119312526361067/zec_s-3_amendment_5.htm

2. *BounceBit will permanently shut its L1 and migrate to BNB Chain after a $3M exploit*
   An Evmos authorization flaw let an attacker move ~286.5M BB (~$3M) with no private key compromised. Rather than patch a chain built on the now-discontinued Evmos stack, BounceBit is winding down its L1 and reissuing BB as a BEP-20 on BNB Chain. A pre-exploit snapshot (block 20,697,260) sets the new distribution; the attacker's 286.5M BB is excluded.
   Why it matters: BB holders must follow the snapshot/migration, not the dying chain — and it's a live warning on app-chains built atop abandoned frameworks.
   https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-08-21-bouncebit-sunset-blockchain-migrate-bnb-chain-after-3-million-exploit-412485

3. *Nvidia to pay Poolside $6B to license its AI model factory, plus a $1B investment*
   Nvidia struck a non-exclusive $6B license to Poolside's coding-model platform, invested $1B at a $12B pre-money valuation, and extended offers to 109 staff — explicitly not an acquisition. NVDA closed the week ~5% lower.
   Why it matters: "license-and-hire" is becoming the template for absorbing AI startups around antitrust — expect copycats, and read the NVDA dip as deal-cost digestion rather than demand softening.
   https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/nvidia-to-pay-ai-startup-poolside-a-6-billion-license-newcomer-says
