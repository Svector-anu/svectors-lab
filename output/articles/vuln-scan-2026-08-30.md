## Threat model (agentic)

Corsair connects apps to third-party APIs with OAuth, webhooks, and optional Hub-managed credential delivery. Highest-value attacker goals: steal tenant OAuth tokens, take over corsair.dev accounts (www uses better-auth magic-link), or abuse MCP `run_script` / forged webhooks / tunnel path-guard bypasses to reach private app routes.
