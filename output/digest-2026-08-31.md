*Daily digest — 2026-08-31*

_TL;DR: VMware packaged private-cloud agent infrastructure while two crypto networks disclosed or contained contract exploits._

1. *Audit migration contracts for replay protection after ICON's exploit*
   ICON says an attacker replayed two signed withdrawal messages 1,492 times, releasing 119.9M ICX and 531,600 bnUSD; user funds were unaffected, but roughly 150.2 ETH and 31,204 USDC remain confirmed losses. The patched network resumed August 28.
   Why it matters: audited bridge and migration code still needs nonce consumption plus automated containment
   https://www.icon.foundation/blog/2026/icon-network-replay-exploit-post-mortem

2. *Cronos halts after an estimated $75M Tectonic exploit*
   Cronos halted its chain after an exploit hit lending protocol Tectonic; the projects had not confirmed the root cause, final loss, or restart timing at publication, while Crypto.com said its app and exchange were unaffected.
   Why it matters: Cronos and Tectonic users should avoid protocol interactions until an official restart and post-mortem
   https://cointelegraph.com/news/cronos-network-halt-tectonic-exploit-75-million

3. *VMware ships a private-cloud stack for production AI agents*
   Broadcom introduced VMware Private AI Cloud and new Tanzu agent tooling with pre-approved skills, workflow buildpacks, human approvals, integrated memory, and governed access to private data.
   Why it matters: enterprise agent teams now have an on-prem path that bundles runtime, data governance, and agent controls
   https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-unveils-ai-ready-data-foundations-vmware-tanzu-platform
