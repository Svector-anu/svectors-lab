>
> same split every cycle: real contract, ticker prices in the news, nobody checks if the contract actually touches the token.

**4b. Narrative**
> read the quant / clearing house release twice looking for the qnt hook. it's not there.
>
> 25 banks, $2t/day network, tokenized deposits going live h1 2027 — quant supplies the interoperability layer. that part's real, and it's a genuine infra win for a project that's spent years building boring plumbing.
>
> then you get to the token. no requirement to hold it, no fee share, no burn, no staking, no supply mechanism mentioned once. the contract is between quant the company and the clearing house. the token is riding along on vibes.
